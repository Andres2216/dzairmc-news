# Google Play release checklist

- Complete the News and Magazine declaration.
- Show publisher contact details inside the app and at a public URL.
- Publish privacy and editorial policies on the developer website.
- Complete Data safety based on the final SDK inventory.
- Declare that the app contains ads.
- Set content rating and target audience accurately.
- Provide current dated content, sources, and publisher attribution.
- Verify `app-ads.txt` through the website listed in the store entry.
- Add the final contact email, public privacy-policy URL, and production AdMob IDs.
- Run a release build and test it on at least two Android versions.
- Upload the AAB to internal testing before closed or production release.
